*   **Action:** Leveraging TOGAF principles, I designed an Enterprise-Scale Landing zone integrating Azure Firewall, advanced Conditional Access (PIM), and isolated subnets. **Project 2: The FinOps Turnaround**
*   **Situation:** A major client was experiencing severe, uncontrolled cloud spend sprawl across unmanaged subscriptions, threatening the ROI of their cloud transformation program.
*   **Task:** Implement immediate cost-saving measures without impacting mission-critical application performance or reliability SLAs.
*   **Action:** I embedded SRE practices directly into their delivery model. I implemented automated right-sizing protocols, established strict Infrastructure as Code (IaC) deployment guardrails, and designed a FinOps operational dashboard for executive oversight.
*   **Result:** Drove an immediate double-digit reduction in monthly cloud expenditure while simultaneously improving system reliability through properly architected autoscaling.

**Project 3: The Multi-Region BFSI Migration Cutover**
*   **Situation:** A high-visibility BFSI client required a complex, multi-region cloud migration with virtually zero tolerance for downtime ("The Hybrid DNS Pattern").
*   **Task:** Design and execute the target state architecture and cutover strategy for highly sensitive financial applications.
*   **Action:** I designed a comprehensive multi-subscription network topology (Hub-and-Spoke with ExpressRoute) to ensure secure hybrid connectivity. I orchestrated the global, asynchronous engineering pods during the critical cutover windows.
*   **Result:** Accelerated the client's digital product launch by 20%, reducing legacy deployment times by 25% through standardized automation and preventing any critical downtime during the transition.
